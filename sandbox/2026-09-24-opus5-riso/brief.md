# opus5-riso · a study of a risograph film

- **Style:** risograph (new style, in testing)
- **Format:** 1:1 · 28 s · 24 fps (the reference is 1080²)
- **Purpose:** a 1:1 study of a riso-printed film, to build the risograph style. **Internal
  study:** someone else's work, not published as ours.
- **Reference:** `references/opus5-risograph.mp4` ([a film by Kevin Ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2099477219877978289),
  [@kevin_t_ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo)),
  copied to `out/reference.mp4` (not committed).
- **The film:** a blue dot on paper; illustrations open one inside another in circles round
  it (a grasshopper on the moon, jellyfish, a bell with an owl, a lighthouse, a wolf, a
  telephone, frogs and the moon, a cat in the rain, a radio, a hummingbird, a waterfall, a
  whale, a rocket, a planet, a volcano, a campfire, mountains…), cutting on the beat; a
  mosaic of all of them in circles that turns blue and scatters; pink and blue orbits; the
  night sky; «opus 5» and «claude» written by hand.

## Measured

- Inks: pink `#e03b84`, yellow `#eee42b`, blue (dots) `#1f9eba`–`#2f76a5`, navy `#1e277b`,
  paper `#f1ebe2`, the dot `#2a2a7d`. Halftone pitch ≈ 9.5 px.
- Cuts: `out/cuts.txt` (`reference.mjs cuts`), 119 cuts, most on eighth notes from 4.2 s.

## Round 1 plan

The lighthouse card (5.58–6.0 s) as the style test; then the other cards, the openings in
circles, the mosaic, the orbits and the title.
