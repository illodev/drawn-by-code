# readme-cover

- **Style:** paper-cutout, with a polaroid of each style painted by its own kit.
- **Format:** 1800 × 680 · 3 s · 24 fps; the last frame is the still cover.
- **Purpose:** the README's cover and the repo's social preview: the repo introduced by
  its own tools.
- **On-screen text (literal):** «drawn by code»; «animation made with code by Claude, and
  every lesson it learned, in the open»; the style names.
- **Must NOT happen:** a hand on backwards or through what it holds; unreadable titles.

## Shot list

| Time | What happens |
