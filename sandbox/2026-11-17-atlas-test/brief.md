# atlas-test

- **Style:** atlas (new: a page of a fine early-20th-century school atlas, Swiss relief)
- **Format:** 16:9 · 12 s · 24 fps
- **Purpose / audience:** the style test of `atlas`. The user asked for «serious, solid
  styles for a new video»; with the video defined as educational («divulgativo»), they asked
  for reference images generated in ElevenLabs. Four directions were generated on one subject
  (how water reaches a city): a Swiss grid, Isotype, an atlas plate and a blueprint; the user
  chose: «Let's go with atlas; don't generate more images with ElevenLabs».
- **One-line idea:** the plate as the reference drew it; then the water leaves the reservoir
  and runs down the canal to the town while the camera follows it, and pulls back to the
  whole plate when it arrives.
- **References:** `ref/atlas-4.png` (the matched plate; ElevenLabs, gpt-image-2.5, the user's
  account) and three more takes of the same prompt, `ref/atlas-1..3.jpg`. The prompt asked
  for «a map in the manner of a fine early-20th-century Swiss school atlas»: shaded relief
  lit from the upper left, hypsometric tints, brown contours, blue water with waterlines, a
  dashed red canal from a dam to a town, serif lettering, a neatline and a scale bar.
- **On-screen text (literal, Spanish, as in the reference):** `EMBALSE`, `Canal`, `CIUDAD`,
  `0 5 10 km`.
- **Music / rhythm:** none yet (a style test).
- **Must NOT happen:** a heightfield seen past its edge; blobby or noisy relief; contours on
  the plain; a light or white patch where the reference had its lettering; a camera so close
  that the measured relief turns soft.
