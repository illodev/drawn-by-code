# light-valley

- **Style:** woodcut (new: a relief print cut in a plank, one black key block and a second
  block in ochre that prints only the electric light)
- **Format:** 16:9 · style test 6 s (the film: about a minute) · 24 fps, drawings on twos
- **Purpose / audience:** the user asked for «serious and solid styles for a new video».
  Eight candidates were shown as a board of generated images; the user chose «the woodcut,
  but not a lighthouse», then the subject «The light comes to the valley» out of three
  proposals, and left the rest to us: «Decide for yourself; the only thing is, don't
  generate any more images with ElevenLabs.»
- **One-line idea:** a mountain valley without electricity; pole by pole the line comes
  down the slope, and every light that comes on is carved out of the black block and
  printed in ochre, until the whole valley is cut.
- **The device:** in a woodcut the light is what is taken away. The film's light is
  literally carved: a lamp or a window that comes on is the gouge going into the block over
  two or three drawings, and the ochre block (the only colour) prints only electric light.
  Before the line arrives there is only black on paper.
- **References** (generated for this film with ElevenLabs, gpt-image-2, by us; in `ref/`):
  - `street-lit.png`: the night the light arrives, the village street with its lamps, the
    family at a lit door (matched in the style test).
  - `valley-night.png`: the valley before electricity (its cut is finer than the one
    chosen for the film).
  - `raising-pole.png`: the line crew raising a pole on the slope, a mule with the cable
    drum: the coarse cut chosen for the whole film.
  - `variants-*.jpg`: the four variants of each, as they were generated.
- **Decisions taken for the user:** the coarse cut of `raising-pole.png` for every shot
  (it is woodcut, not wood engraving, it reads at speed and after compression); the ochre
  block only for electric light; the moon goes from a crescent to full over the film (the
  weeks the line takes).
- **On-screen text (literal):** none in the style test.
- **Music / rhythm:** to be decided with the user (the style test is silent).
- **Must NOT happen:** a light that glows instead of being carved; any colour but ochre
  (and the ochre never on anything the electric light does not touch); fine engraved
  hatching (that is the engraving style); lens-shaped «eyes» for stones; ovals of flat
  ochre for pools of light; a lighthouse.
