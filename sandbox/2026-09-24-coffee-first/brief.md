# coffee-first

- **Style:** paper-cutout
- **Format:** 16:9 · 6 s · 24 fps · 120 BPM
- **What for / for whom:** first experiment in the repo: check that the engine, the paper
  kit and the review loop work on their own, with a new character.
- **Idea in one sentence:** a sleeping mug is woken up by its own steam.
- **References:** the earlier paper-cutout style test.
- **On-screen text (literal):** «Primero, café.» ("Coffee first.")
- **Music / rhythm:** no music; cuts and hits on a 120 BPM grid.
- **What must NOT happen:** boiling outlines, gradient steam, nothing still for more than 1 s.

## Shot by shot

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Table | The mug falls onto the table on the 0.5 s beat, squashes and bounces. It sleeps. |
| 2–4 | Steam | Three undulating paper steam strips rise; at 3.0 the mug opens one eye, at 3.5 both. |
| 4–6 | Phrase | A ruled paper card comes in and «Primero, café.» is written, underlined. The mug winks at 5.0. |
