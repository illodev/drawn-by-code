# souvenir-test · «Recuerdo de Andalucía»

- **Style:** souvenir (new, in testing)
- **Format:** 16:9 · 8 s style test (the film it tests would be about 14 s) · 24 fps
- **Purpose / audience:** a style test for a new look the user asked for.
- **The request (the user, translated):** «I want to make similar art, but not the same.
  It is only the reference for the style.» Asked what world ours should paint: «Andalusia,
  and deep references, some harder to understand and others less so. Magic, enchantment
  (embrujo), rootedness, culture, poetry, art, and so on. We can debate it.» Asked about
  human figures: none for now.
- **One-line idea:** a camera dives through five nested paintings of Andalusia, each
  inside a hole of the one before, while the moon grows from a crescent to full.
- **Reference:** a 15.6 s screen capture of a video game's intro sequence (Rockstar
  Games), given by the user for its style only. It is third-party footage: it lives in
  `out/reference.mp4` (not committed), none of its content is reproduced (no logos,
  characters, compositions or lettering), and nothing here is published as theirs.
- **On-screen text (literal, Spanish):** the ribbon: `SONIDOS NEGROS`. A guitar maker's
  label inside the sound hole (small, decorative).
- **Music / rhythm:** none yet. The reference's own sound is not used.
- **Must NOT happen:** anything of the reference's content; people (for now); flat vector
  fills; a logo that is not ours.

## What the reference does (measured on its frames)

- **Structure:** one continuous dive of about 8 s through five painted pictures, each
  inside a hole of the one before (an emblem, fruit, a jungle, a postcard behind arches, a
  collage lit by fire), then a 0.75 s rewind through all of them and a logo. Per picture:
  arrive decelerating, a slow push of about half a second, accelerate into the hole.
- **Five manners of paint, one per picture:** dark contour + airbrush (the emblem); brush
  without line (the fruit); near-monochrome with one saturated subject (the jungle); pastel
  with gold ornament and stone (the postcard); pulp painting with rim light over a sticker
  collage (the fire).
- **Colour:** one dominant hue family per picture. Blacks are lifted: the darkest 5 % of
  the frame sits at 0.21–0.43 luminance, the lightest 5 % at 0.73–0.85 (nothing clips).
  Saturation is moderate (median 0.25–0.53).
- **Camera:** layers in depth; what is near is strongly out of focus; a radial smear while
  crossing a hole; a soft glow on the lights (a mist filter).

The through-line is **the moon, waxing from picture to picture** until it is full in the forge.
Every film ends with the signature `illodev x Claude`.

## The script after the user's second round (to debate)

The user: more detail at every pixel; the history of Andalusia and its civilizations;
its poets, artists and faiths (those who were born here, passed through or chose it);
darker, almost mystical parts and lighter ones, abstract parts and realistic ones, hidden
references; the length does not matter. So the dive grows and alternates:

| # | Picture | Light and manner | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The emblem** | indigo night, dense, the emblem manner | flamenco; a string of coins, one per people who passed; a wheel of relics from Tartessos to al-Andalus; the books of its poets; objects of the three faiths |
| 2 | **The lemon tree** | bright, realistic, brush | Machado's orchard, the blossom, a goldfinch |
| 3 | **Green** | a dream, half abstract | the whole «Romance sonámbulo», as ghosts |
| 4 | **The still life** | tenebrist, hyper-real | the Seville of Velázquez and Zurbarán: the water seller's jug with its drops, the pots on a ledge, Valdés Leal's candle |
| 5 | **The dark night** | the blackest, mystical | St John of the Cross (the flame, his Mount Carmel), Ibn Arabi's circles, the candles of the small hours of Holy Week |
| 6 | **The dome** | pure geometry, light from above | the Alhambra's muqarnas, the Byzantine gold of Córdoba's mihrab |
| 7 | **The patio** | dawn pastels, realistic | arches, flowerpots, the confronted peacocks of Madinat al-Zahra |
| 8 | **The guitar broken into planes** | abstract, a homage to Cubism | Málaga; the guitar of the first picture, taken apart (no existing work is copied) |
| 9 | **The forge and the moon** | fire | «La luna vino a la fragua»; the full moon, the signature |

An alternative for the order, also to debate: the dive as a descent through time, each
picture an older layer, down to Tartessos.

## How it is built

- `styles/souvenir/souvenir.js`: the paint (a per-pixel form painter for the brush manner,
  pieces with contour and airbrush for the emblem manner), the dive rig (layers at depths,
  depth of field, motion blur by open shutter) and the lens finish.
- `tableaux/emblem.js`, `tableaux/lemons.js`: each picture as a stack of layers.
- `things/*.js`: one file per group of elements (fan, guitar, garland, sky, ribbon, charms,
  shawl, citrus), painted once into sprites; only the camera moves.
- The elements of the emblem were painted in parallel from `agent-brief.md`.
