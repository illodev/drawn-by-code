# tide · «Marea»

- **Style:** sand (new): sand animation on a light table
- **Format:** 16:9 · ~45 s · 24 fps (style test: 7 s)
- **Purpose / audience:** a showcase short for the new style; the technique is part of the story
- **One-line idea:** the tide wipes the beach clean, and every time someone builds the castle again.
- **References:** the craft of sand animation on a light table (Kseniya Simonova, Ferenc Cakó): warm light from below, thick sand near black, thin sand speckled and amber, finger lines as channels of light with piled rims, palm sweeps that leave a ridge and striations, poured heaps ringed with loose grains, no cuts (every picture turns into the next).
- **On-screen text (literal):** none in the pictures; two initials written with a finger in the sand in the noon scene.
- **Music / rhythm:** slow, 60 BPM; a sparse acoustic piece (guitar or piano) with the sea under it, and the sand's own sounds (a pour, a finger, the sweep as the wave).
- **Must NOT happen:** a cut (the medium transforms, it never cuts); flat greys (thin sand is grains with light between them); a sweep that reads as a wipe effect; people drawn as outlines (sand people are masses with details scratched out).

## The idea

One person's life on one beach, told by the sand itself. Each wave is the artist's palm
sweeping the table: it takes the picture away and leaves the next one behind it. The
light under the glass is the time of day, and the time of day is the time of life.
The object that changes: the sandcastle, built again in every age, bigger each time it is
built together.

## Shot list (whole film, draft)

| Time | Shot | Light | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Shore | dawn | The empty glass glows; sand pours along the bottom into the beach, a finger draws the sea's lines. |
| 4–11 | Child | morning | A child crouches and builds a castle: each tower is a poured heap that rises, the finger presses its windows and draws the flag; a gull flies over on twos. |
| 11–13 | Wave | | A wave crosses the table and takes castle and child with it. |
| 13–20 | Two | noon | Two trails of footprints walk in from the sides and meet; the two sit down; a finger writes two initials in the wet sand. |
| 20–22 | Wave | | The wave takes the initials. |
| 22–30 | Parent | afternoon | The same person, grown, kneels with a small child: they build the castle together, bigger than the first. |
| 30–32 | Wave | | |
| 32–40 | Old | sunset | An old person with a stick sits by the water while a grandchild builds; the old figure slowly blows away into grains on the wind. |
| 40–45 | Again | night → dawn | The last wave stops short of the castle; the light comes back to dawn; the child alone with the castle, as at the start. |
