# what-do-you-love · 1:1 replica

- **Style:** paper-cutout (a "picture book" variant: lighter and sweeter than our earlier short)
- **Format:** 1:1 · 28 s · 24 fps (the reference is 2160×2160)
- **Purpose:** frame-by-frame replica exercise of a paper-cutout reference video, to measure
  how far the style and the engine go. **Internal use only:** it is someone else's work; it
  is not published as ours.
- **Reference:** [a video by Kevin Ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2102437977435893771)
  ([@kevin_t_ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo)): the design and animation are his, this is a
  study. Supplied by the user (`out/reference.mp4`, not committed). Sheets every
  0.25 s with `node engine/reference.mjs sheets out/reference.mp4`.
- **On-screen text (literal):** «what do you love?», and in the montage: words, music, the
  sea, trees, dogs, bread, rain, math, the stars, octopus, tea, flowers, cats.
- **Typeface:** Short Stack (OFL), the closest free match; drawn with a second translucent
  pass so it reads as marker.
- **Music:** the reference's own track, used locally only for sync (`out/reference-audio.wav`).

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Night exterior | House on the left with the girl writing at her window, town on the right with a cat on a roof; paper planes rise towards the sun-flower (top right), which shakes with little ticks at 0.5. |
| 1.5–2 | Interior | The girl lifts her pencil with an idea ("o" mouth, ticks) and at 1.75 closes her eyes and smiles. |
| 2–3 | Notepad, top-down | The arm writes «what do». |
| 3–3.75 | Notepad, close | Continues «you love»; at 3.75 medium shot with the full «what do you love?». |
| 4–4.5 | Tear | Both hands tear the page off; bits of paper jump. |
| 4.5–5.25 | Fold | Over a circle of light, the hands fold the sheet into a plane: flat, triangle, plane. |
| 5.25–6 | Interior | She throws the plane up to the right (motion lines). |
| 6–7 | Open sky | The plane rises from bottom left towards the flower, with a dotted trail; town below. |
| 7–7.5 | Flower, close | The plane hits the flower ("o" mouth), which catches it and unfolds it. |
| 7.5–9.75 | Flower reads | Holds the note «what do you love?», closes its eyes to think (bubbles 8.25–8.75), «!» at 9.0, bounces happily. |
| 10–15.75 | Montage | 0.5 s cards (the last ones 0.25 s) with the flower and what it loves: words, music, the sea, trees, dogs, bread, rain, math, the stars (1 s), octopus, tea, flowers, cats. The word writes itself in. |
| 16–18 | Heart | Torn pink heart with the flower in the middle; everything from the montage appears around it one by one; at 17.75 a blue «?» and a small red heart. |
| 18–20 | Flower answers | Turns the note over, folds it into a plane and throws it down to the left. |
| 20–21 | Exterior | The plane comes down to the window and the girl catches it. |
| 21–22 | Interior | She unfolds and reads it (the back shows through mirrored, with «you» circled in red). |
| 22–22.75 | Note, close | «what do (you) love?», «you» circled in red and a little flower drawn on it: the answer is "you". |
| 22.75–24 | Interior | Surprise ("o"), smile, closed eyes, hearts: she hugs the note. |
| 24–26 | Exterior | The flower lights little lights in the town; the girl waves. |
| 26–27 | Interior | She pins the note on the wall next to the flower drawing and hugs herself. |
| 27–28 | Exterior | Like the opening: the planes rise again; loop. |
