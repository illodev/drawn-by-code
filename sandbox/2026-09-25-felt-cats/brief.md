# felt-cats · brief

**What:** the «dancing cowboy cats» meme (three kittens dancing on a green screen; the
reference is the green-screen version shared by MemeScreens) replicated 1:1 in its dance,
in a new style: needle-felted wool puppets (`styles/felt3d`).

**The idea:** the same three cats and the same dance on any set, as TikTok does with the
green-screen meme: the cats render with alpha and cast their shadow on the backdrop's
floor; a set is one module in `backdrops.js` (paint + light).

**Format:** 9:16, 15.84 s, 30 fps, stop motion on twos (15 drawings/s). Music at 123 BPM:
our own track (the reference's audio stays in `out/`, never in the repo).

**Cast:** cat 0 cream-orange fold (folded ears); cat 1 white with a black felt cowboy hat
and a silver band; cat 2 white with a black helmet, back patches, black tail, round wire
glasses.

**Rights:** the reference video and its audio live only in `out/` (gitignored); pose data
read off it stays in `private/`. What is committed is the kit, the cats, the sets and the
choreography authored from measurements.

**Not allowed:** the original audio in any committed render; a dance «inspired by» instead
of measured.
