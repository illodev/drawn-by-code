# pier-pressure

First of a series: **the opening titles of cartoons that never existed**, one per drawing
style. Same structure every time (the place, each character with a name card and a gag, a
group finale, the title), so the styles can be compared.

- **Style:** skate-toon (new: the look of a late-'90s extreme-sports TV cartoon, from five
  frames of the genre the user gave as a reference for the *style*; cast, place and title are
  ours)
- **Format:** 16:9 · 20–30 s · 24 fps · drawings on twos
- **Purpose / audience:** a short for the repo's gallery and for social; people who grew up
  with those cartoons
- **One-line idea:** the opening of «Pier Pressure»: four kids on wheels in a cold-water surf
  town, an aunt who fries the chips and a gull who takes his cut.
- **References:** the user's five frames (not committed)
- **Language:** English on screen, English names (the user's choice)
- **On-screen text (literal):** `PIER PRESSURE` (title) · name cards `KIT`, `HOAGIE`, `MILO`,
  `PIP`, `AUNT DOT`, `CAPTAIN` · set lettering `DOT'S CHIPS`, `GULL HARBOR`
- **Music / rhythm:** a 25 s surf-punk theme at about 120 BPM, instrumental with a shouted
  title at the end (to generate, skill **sound**); cuts on the beat
- **Must NOT happen:** anything of an existing show (characters, logo, place names, jersey
  designs); shading; a pose or a hand at less than the model sheet's level

## The place: Gull Harbor

A fishing and surf town on a cold coast, under the genre's yellow sky: a stone quay with
bollards and a boat ramp the kids use as a launch, a sea wall, Dot's chip hut with a striped
awning and a hand-painted sign, a lighthouse on the point, cranes and trawlers behind, a
wooden pier with a rail to grind and an end to jump off.

## The cast (model sheet: `review/cast.jpg`)

| Who | Silhouette | Does | The joke |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kit**, 11 | a bean of a head, lime hair in two stiff bunches, a plaster on the nose, a gap in her grin, a hooped tee, yellow high-tops | skates; first one down | never admits she is scared |
| **Hoagie**, 10 | a head wider than tall, a buzz cut with one cowlick, braces, a Hawaiian shirt | films everything on the family camcorder | narrates like a commentator and never gets the trick in frame |
| **Milo**, 12 | a tall narrow head, a blue flat-top, round glasses mended with tape, endless legs in tube socks | tunes the boards with bits from the harbour | works it all out and falls anyway |
| **Pip**, 8 | a helmet two sizes too big covered in stickers, two braids, dungarees | Kit's sister, on a scooter | better than all of them and not bothered |
| **Aunt Dot** | a square jaw, a visor, a bun with a pencil through it, an apron | ex-surfer, runs the chip hut | the adult who lets them get on with it |
| **Captain** | a one-eyed gull with a patch | steals chips | closes every gag |

## Shot list (proposal, 24 s at 120 BPM: to agree with the user)

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Gull Harbor | Captain, on a bollard, watches Dot roll up the hut's shutter; the camera drops along the quay |
| 2–5 | KIT | Kit drops off the sea wall on her board and lands in front of us; her name slams in |
| 5–8 | HOAGIE | Hoagie pans his camcorder after her a beat too late; his viewfinder shows only sky |
| 8–11 | MILO | Milo gives a truck one last turn of the spanner; the board shoots off without him |
| 11–14 | PIP | Pip overtakes all three on her scooter without looking up from her lollipop |
| 14–17 | AUNT DOT | Dot slides four paper cones of chips down the counter, one to each |
| 17–20 | CAPTAIN | the gull swoops along the counter and takes a chip from every cone in one pass |
| 20–24 | Title | the four chase him down the pier and off its end; they hang in the air, the title stamps on |
