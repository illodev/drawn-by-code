# pyramid-engraving · brief

**What:** «The pyramid that makes skies» (60 s), from the user's scripts document: a camera
explores a pyramid that wakes up, opens into thousands of floating blocks and shows an alien
machine that grows a seed of universe and sends it to the sky as a new star.

**Style:** a new one, `styles/engraving`: the pyramid printed as a copperplate engraving,
after the plates of the «Description de l'Égypte» (1809–1822), which the user picked from
references: burin lines whose width is the tone, a ruled sky, laid paper, blue as the only
live ink and gold as a wash. A WebGL rasteriser, so thousands of instanced blocks are cheap.

**First step (style test):** a still of the decisive shot, PIR-03, the impossible exploded
view held at 14–15.5 s, full bleed and inside a printed plate, plus the closed pyramid.

**Rules from the script:** the silhouette stays a pyramid while it opens; every block keeps
its volume and moves along a direction set by its face; the machine is already working
when the exterior opens; three quarters of the frame stay mineral or dark; the invented
building is not a real pyramid (no real names on the plate).
