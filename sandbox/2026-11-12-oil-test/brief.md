# oil-test

- **Style:** oil (new, in testing)
- **Format:** 16:9 · 8 s · 24 fps, painted on twos
- **Purpose / audience:** a style test for a film told in the first person: an old man with
  macular degeneration. He has lost the centre of his sight, so whatever he looks at
  straight on is gone, and his memory fills the hole (Charles Bonnet syndrome). The test
  has to show whether oil paint can carry that rule before the film is written.
- **One-line idea:** his eye crosses a grey winter square and, wherever it rests, the
  summer of 1974 is painted back; it stops at a door where she is standing.
- **The rule of the film:** one paint, two brushes. The present is painted out of focus
  with wide brushes and never finished; the memory, inside the patch where he looks, is the
  same square finished with small brushes in clean colour. The patch follows his eye, is
  blocked in at its rim and finished at its centre, and loosens again behind it.
- **References:** ten paintings by Monet sent by the user (uncommitted, in `private/ref/`):
  the self-portrait with a beret, the Japanese footbridge, The Luncheon, Woman with a
  Parasol, Woman in the Garden, the Tuileries, a walk at Argenteuil, Vétheuil, The Magpie.
  Measured for tonal range, the colour of the darks and how broken the colour is (see
  `review.md`).
- **On-screen text (literal):** none.
- **Music / rhythm:** none in the test. The film will be led by his voice.
- **Must NOT happen:** black in the shadows; a photographic blur (the present is soft
  because it is painted soft); a clean geometric edge on the patch; anything sharp in the
  present.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0.5 | The square, now | An overcast morning out of focus: a bare plane tree, a shut shop with a car parked in front, a door. Nothing is sharp. |
| 0.5–1.5 | The tree | Where he is looking, summer opens: the tree comes into leaf, the village behind it lights up, a blue bicycle leans on the trunk. |
| 1.5–3.1 | Across the square | His eye travels right and the head follows late; the summer travels with it and the tree goes bare again behind. |
| 3.1–4.3 | The shop | The shutter and the car give way to the shop open under its green and cream awning: tins on the shelves, crates of fruit in the sun. |
| 4.3–5.5 | To the door | The eye moves on to the house door. |
| 5.5–8 | Her | She stands in the doorway in a pale dress, a basket on her arm, a hand shading her eyes, looking this way. He holds his eye on her. |

Depth order, back to front: sky · far houses and church tower · garden wall and
bougainvillea · ground · the building's wall, string course and plinth · balconies ·
shop (interior, shelves, counter, posts, crates, awning) · door · lamp · tree (shade on the
ground, trunk, crown) · bicycle in front of the trunk · her in front of the open leaf ·
(now only) the car in front of the shop.
