# exquisite-corpse

- **Style:** several, one per segment: paper-cutout → 70s-poster → liquid-light → kaleidoscope → line → paper-cutout
- **Format:** 16:9 · 30 s · 24 fps · 120 BPM (beat = 0.5 s)
- **What for / for whom:** test of a psychedelic video that jumps between styles without
  losing the thread. It also serves to debut four styles and the transitions skill.
- **Idea in one sentence:** a colorful marble passes from creature to creature and from
  style to style, like the relay of an exquisite corpse, until it comes back to the start.
- **References:** *Rick and Morty · Exquisite Corpse* (Adult Swim). What we take: each
  segment in a different style, the action that continues through the change and the
  transitions that carry it from one style to another (entering through the eye or the
  mouth, engulf, vortex, frame within the frame). None of its characters.
- **On-screen text (literal):** «¡GLUP!» (70s poster; "GULP!"). Nothing else: it is visual.
- **Music / rhythm:** 120 BPM. For now, effects from `assets/sfx` and a synthesized pulse.
- **What must NOT happen:** characters from existing brands or shows (e.g. Rick and
  Morty); hard cuts between styles; losing sight of the marble during a change; a
  segment that looks like the same style in another color.

**Common thread:** the marble, a small sphere with swirls of color, always the most
saturated thing in the shot. Every transition passes **through** it or whoever holds it.

## Shot by shot

| Time | Segment · style | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | **The frog** · paper cutout | Pond at night. A paper frog dozes on its lily pad. The marble floats down like a firefly (1.5). The frog opens one eye (2.5) and **catches it with its tongue on the 3.5 beat**. It swallows (4.0), its eyes turn into colorful spirals (4.5). |
| 5–6.5 | *Transition · enter through the eye* | The camera goes into the eye's spiral; inside, the next world opens up. |
| 6–12 | **The mushroom** · 70s poster | Sunbeams spinning in orange, magenta and acid green. A mushroom with a wavy outline dances to the beat. «¡GLUP!» drops and melts (6.5–8). The marble bounces on the mushroom's cap on every beat (8–10). The mushroom sneezes (10.5). |
| 10.5–12 | *Transition · engulf* | The spores from the sneeze grow like clouds and fill the screen: they are already the blobs of the next segment. |
| 12–18 | **The jellyfish** · liquid light | Oil and ink blobs that flow and merge. A jellyfish forms from them (12.5–14), with the marble glowing inside the bell, and drifts. The colors rotate. |
| 16.5–18 | *Transition · vortex* | Everything spirals toward the marble and shrinks to a point. |
| 18–23 | **The climax** · kaleidoscope | From the point a kaleidoscope of 8 mirrors opens, made of pieces of everything before (frog eyes, mushroom caps, tentacles, marbles). It spins and changes hue on the 20.5 beat. |
| 22–23.5 | *Transition · frame within the frame* | The camera pulls back: the kaleidoscope was a drawing inside a circle on a sheet of paper. |
| 23–28.5 | **The doodle** · line on white | A wobbly black-line character looks at his drawing, which turns black and white. Only the marble keeps its color. It rolls away across the sheet; he chases it, jumps and catches it (26.5). He crumples the sheet (27.5). |
| 28–30 | *Transition · paper ball → back to the start* | The paper ball unfolds into the frog's pond and the marble comes down again: the ending fits the beginning in a loop. |
