# clay3d-test

- **Style:** clay3d (new style, in testing): a raymarched 3D plasticine set.
- **Format:** 16:9 · 4 s · 24 fps, stop motion on twos.
- **Purpose / audience:** the user asked whether we could reach the look of classic
  claymation (a still of a well-known clay film as the reference for the LOOK only: its
  characters are someone else's and are not copied). A first test of the 3D route.
- **One-line idea:** Laura at a table with a cloth, a mug in both hands, the brand's cloud
  on a clay plaque; she looks at it, turns to the camera, raises her mug with a grin.
- **References:** the user's still (camera at table height, background out of focus, soft
  shadows, glossy eyes, knitted wool, a tablecloth, wallpaper).
- **On-screen text (literal):** none.
- **Music / rhythm:** none in the test; 120 BPM grid for the contacts (toast at 1.92).
- **Must NOT happen:** copying the reference's characters; a logo that is not the client's
  (the plaque's cloud uses the lobes of the site's SVG); hands through the mug.
