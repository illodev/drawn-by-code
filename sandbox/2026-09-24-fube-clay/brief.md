# fube-clay

- **Style:** clay (new style, in testing)
- **Format:** 16:9 · 6 s · 24 fps
- **Purpose / audience:** a style exploration for the saas-promo marketing video: the same
  approved moment (the invoice block, 10–16 s of that edit) redone in plasticine, to compare
  side by side with the paper-cutout version.
- **One-line idea:** Laura issues an invoice with one press and it flies, stamped, into the
  tax office's mailbox.
- **References:** the saas-promo paper version (`sandbox/2026-09-24-saas-promo/render/`),
  claymation in the Aardman tradition (big white eyes, chunky four-finger hands, visible
  fingerprints, a lit table-top set).
- **On-screen text (literal):** the client's copy from `BRAND.copy` (the private brand of
  saas-promo): the invoice UI, «issued», «compliance».
- **Music / rhythm:** the saas-promo mix, 10–16 s (120 BPM); every contact on the same
  beat as the paper version: press 1.0, stamp 2.5, fold 3.0, flight 3.5–4.5, flag 4.5.
- **Must NOT happen:** hands through things or on backwards; a logo that is not the
  client's; flat vector look (every piece has volume, texture and a shadow).
