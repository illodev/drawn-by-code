## Round 1

- Auto: no warnings, warm frame 24 ms. By eye at full size:
  - the arms came out with «wings» and the fingers like toes: `Clay.capsule` swept its
    caps inwards (fixed in the kit); an arm is now two sausages (upper arm, forearm);
  - small pieces (hands) washed out: the bevel is capped at 14 % of the piece;
  - the rest hands showed only nails: the fingers now lie ON the back of the hand;
  - the laptop lid read as a cushion: thin slab, small bevel, a hinge;
  - the half-folded sheet looked like a leaf: straight edges subdivided before `lumpy`;
  - the plane now goes into the slot (clipped at its lip), not over the mailbox;
  - the set was bare at the bottom: a drawer, a notebook and a foreground plant.
- Sent to the user with a side-by-side against the paper version (private/paper-vs-clay.mp4).
