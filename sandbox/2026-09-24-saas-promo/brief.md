# saas-promo

A marketing video for a client's invoicing product. The client's copy, logo, fonts and the
full script with its sources live in `private/` (never committed: `sandbox/*/private/`).
This file keeps the craft side only.

- **Style:** paper-cutout, at the detail bar of the what-do-you-love replica
- **Format:** 16:9 · 50 s · 24 fps · 120 BPM (cuts on 0.5 s)
- **Purpose / audience:** website and YouTube; small businesses and freelancers
- **One-line idea:** the paperwork of a small business, scattered across paper,
  spreadsheets, email, the bank and the accountant, is gathered into one place and starts
  working for you.
- **Through-line:** a desk buried in paper that the brand's cloud swallows and sorts,
  block by block, until the desk is clean.
- **On-screen text:** only literal lines from the client's website (see private brief).
- **Music / rhythm:** music + paper sound effects, no voice-over.
- **Must NOT happen:** promises the website doesn't make; the product's UI shown as a flat
  screenshot (it is printed on paper, like everything else); boiling outlines.

## Shot list (generic)

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | Chaos | Papers rain onto the desk until they bury the owner up to the eyes |
| 6–10 | Cloud | The brand cloud descends and swallows the pile |
| 10–16 | Invoice | «Issue» is pressed: the invoice prints with its QR, folds and flies into the tax office's mailbox |
| 16–21 | Expenses | A phone photographs a receipt; its numbers jump into their fields |
| 21–27 | Collections | An overdue invoice rings a bell; the reminder waits for approval, then flies as a paper plane |
| 27–33 | Taxes | Each invoice that drops in fills the quarterly tax envelopes; calendar pages turn |
| 33–38 | Business | A question to the assistant grows into per-client margin bars |
| 38–43 | Compliance | The 2027 deadlines on the calendar; the invoice gets its compliance QR stamp |
| 43–50 | Close | The clean desk, a coffee, logo, closing lines and the web address |
