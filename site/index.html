Postbay · self-hosted · MIT

A studio for everything after the render.

A film is not finished when it is rendered: somebody has to look at it, ask for changes, agree it is ready and send it out. Postbay is where that happens, for your team and your AI agents, on your own server.

Visit postbay.app The code on GitHub
On the exact frame
Comment on a moment or a span of a video, draw on the frame, compare two versions side by side.
Bound to the exact file
An approval belongs to one version’s files: change a byte and it no longer counts.
Nine networks
A calendar that publishes by itself, or gets everything ready for a person to post.
Pairs with drawn-by-code
It is an MCP server for Claude. A request for changes on a film from this repo becomes its next version: the agent edits the scene, renders it again and uploads it.
